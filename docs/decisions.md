# Decision log

Every row is a decision that was made and confirmed. The numbers are fixed:
D24, D25, D28 to D32, D59, D76, D113 and D122 to D124 are not in this log, and are left unused so that
the numbers other documents quote do not change. Rows D1 to D58 are product and
design decisions. Rows D60 to D129 are engineering decisions made during the
build.

"Where in code" names the file or test that implements the decision today.
"Not built yet" means nothing in the repository implements it so far. A table
name such as `payment_attempts` is defined in `prisma/schema.prisma` and its
migration, even where the code that uses the table does not exist yet.

The one-sentence reasons were written when this log was compiled. They state why
the decision helps; they are not quotes from the people who decided.
